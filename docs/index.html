Crownwork

Pruning trainer for fruiting plants

Learn where to cut—and what the plant does next.

Crownwork is an interactive pruning trainer for North American fruiting plants. Start with apple on a central-leader tree, switch modes for berries, vines, and other woody crops, and toggle Advanced to see before-and-after physiology with short citations.

Mode
Detail

Scroll or pinch to zoom. Drag to pan. Tab to cut markers; Enter selects. Escape closes the detail panel.

Cut detail

Select a labeled marker on the plant to see how to make the cut and why it matters. Switch to Advanced for before-and-after physiology and sources.

  • ● Heading — shorten a shoot to thicken and branch
  • ● Thinning — remove a limb at its origin
  • ● Renewal — replace aging fruiting wood
  • ● Sucker — remove watersprouts and root suckers
